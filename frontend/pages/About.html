Born from a cosmic boredom in the Shinigami realm and a insatiable mortal thirst for occult artifacts and high-stakes justice.

How It Began

Centuries ago, gods of death gambled bones and stared at the grey skies of an unchanging realm. Ryuk, seeking amusement, dropped a single leather-bound notebook into the human world. That experiment reshaped mortal civilization.

Recognizing humanity's fascination with divine judgment, dark aesthetic relics, detective psychology, and gothic couture, the Shinigami Council established Death Mart: the only sanctioned cross-dimensional emporium.

Today, Death Mart delivers exact replica notebooks, feather quills, high-caffeine sugar confections, detective wardrobe essentials, and handcrafted figures directly to mortal doorsteps across India and the globe.
